# wirenook

> Cassidy Prather's personal website. The reviews and essays here are the source of truth for Cassidy's tastes: what they like, what they don't, and why. If you are working on one of Cassidy's games, read them before guessing.

Everything below is an index rather than a single post, so it stays current as posts are added. The blog's full-text JSON is the quickest way to read or search all of it in one request. The site is static and has no Markdown copies of its pages; the JSON is the plain-text version.

## Opinions

- [Blog]({{ "blog/" | absURL }}): every post, newest first. Essays, reviews, and an archive of Cassidy's cohost posts from 2022 to 2024.
- [Full-text index]({{ "blog/index.json" | absURL }}): every blog post as JSON, one object each, with `title`, `url`, `date`, and `text` (the whole post as plain text).
- [Reviews]({{ "blog/tags/review/" | absURL }}): video games, books, visual novels and board games. Steam, Goodreads and VNDB reviews are mirrored here from where they were written; the board game dates are approximate.
- [Reflections]({{ "blog/tags/reflection/" | absURL }}): one post per game Cassidy has made, looking back on it. These live here and only here; a game's repository links to its reflection instead of keeping a copy.

## Games

- [Games]({{ $games }}): Cassidy's games, playable in the browser behind one menu.
- [Source](https://github.com/CassidyPrather/game-prototypes): the repository those games are built and deployed from.

## Style

- [Style guide]({{ "style/" | absURL }}): wirenook's visual identity. Logos, palette, typefaces, marks and wallpapers, under the designer's brief.

## Optional

A few posts that are about game design directly:

- [Agency vs. Replayability]({{ "blog/agency-vs-replayability/" | absURL }}): on games that pile on hand-made reactivity, and the replaying-for-completion it drives.
- [Rapid prototyping is cool]({{ "blog/rapid-proto-good/" | absURL }}): a design note from Space Trucking, on dropping a "start with few things" progression constraint that left the opening empty.
- [Pathologic is the Best Video Game Ever]({{ "blog/cohost/pathologic-is-the-best-video-game-ever/" | absURL }}): Cassidy's favourite game, and why.
- [Autonomy is Trash]({{ "blog/cohost/autonomy-is-trash/" | absURL }}): on generated content and total freedom in interactive media; generating it isn't the point, engaging with it is.
{{- end }}
