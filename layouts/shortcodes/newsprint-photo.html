{{- /* A picture in the paper, for a post wearing themes/fake-news/ — or the box that holds its place until there is one. Called with no `src`, this prints an empty plate at the aspect ratio the finished art will have, cross-ruled, carrying the mark a paste-up would: TK, the newsroom's "to come". That is the whole reason the shortcode takes a caption independently of a source — the caption can be written, and the page laid out around the space, before the photograph exists. Filling in `src` later turns the same call into the real thing and nothing else in the file changes. Params: src Path under images/, as mounted into assets. Omit for a placeholder. alt Alt text; pass "" for a decorative image, as img requires. caption The line under the plate. credit Who the picture belongs to, set at the end of the caption. ratio Aspect ratio of the placeholder, as a CSS value. Default 3 / 2. Ignored once there is a `src`, which brings its own dimensions. span "all" (default) for lead art across the whole sheet, or "column" for a picture that sits inside one column of the story.
