"Agreeable issues" are things that are:
1. Completely uncontroversial
2. Benefit everyone
3. Low risk

## Mostly Agreeable Issues

How do you enforce that?
The thing about money is that it's extremely liquid, you know?
But at the same time, it's actionable *enough* in so many ways that it makes sense...
