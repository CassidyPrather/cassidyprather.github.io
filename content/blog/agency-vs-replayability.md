---
title: "Agency vs. Replayability"
date: 2026-08-16T15:45:00-07:00
---

Some well-made games (e.g. [Stardew Valley](https://www.stardewvalley.net/))
invest **heavily** in manually adding content
to enhance the illusion of player agency (or "reactivity").
Romance routes, story endings, strategies, terrain generation presets...
I can't speak to whether this is a good investment
(many of my friends actively *dislike* when visual novels give them choices),
but I can't help but notice
that this investment seems to have an inexorable side effect
of causing people to re-play the game *in absolute **droves***
to "experience all the content",
even when most of the game is moment-per-moment identical.
That's a strikingly depressing state of affairs!
