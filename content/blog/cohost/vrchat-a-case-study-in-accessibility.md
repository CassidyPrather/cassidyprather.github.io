This week, VRChat rolled out changes limiting [maximum download and uncompressed avatar sizes.](https://docs.vrchat.com/docs/latest-release)

This has been... Controversial.

<!--more-->

https://x.com/LiindyVR/status/1773342271846363366?s=20

https://x.com/kentrl_z/status/1773454369867878438?s=20

_The discourse has been endless, I just like these examples._

Now, all of my friends know firmly where I stand on this. From incessant nagging about the [application of content warnings](https://hello.vrchat.com/blog/content-gating) to impromptu voice-call workshops on [Easy VRAM management](https://creators.vrchat.com/avatars/avatar-optimizing-tips/#watch-your-vram-usage), all the way to pulling teeth to get people to upload [quest-compatible avatars](https://creators.vrchat.com/platforms/android/cross-platform-setup/) and [setting palatable fallbacks](https://docs.vrchat.com/docs/avatar-fallback-system)... I will never shut up about VRChat accessibility.

One thing I've noticed, being this insufferable, is that *people don't intrinsically want to make accessible content*. The default will always be to take the past of least resistance- and if other people can't see their avatars, have their PCs nuked by glorious 4k textures, or are exposed to obscene content, well... "Get a PC! Get a _better_ PC! Kids shouldn't be on the internet, anyway! Not my responsibility!"

And well... That's depressing! What a horrid thought! But it got me thinking, how do people make _anything_ accessible, if people don't want to do that? In real life, we have disabled people, right? Or colorblind people, or- hell- impoverished people without the ability to dunk funds on all the niceties of boogie living, trapped in a cycle of poor mens' shoes.

Well, I guess they did the same thing VRChat's doing. That is to say: Forcing people. Get accessible or get #&$*'d In my home country, for example, it was through the [Americans with Disabilities Act](https://www.ada.gov/). Why'd that pass? I'm no political scholar, and I can't speculate. But now, I'm really desperate to know how they did it.
