<div>I had a really strange moment the other night hanging out with my pal Mari. She shared <details style="display:inline-flex;padding:0 0.3em;background:#83254F;color:#ffffff;box-shadow: 0px 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,.2);border-radius: 0.5rem">

<div style="background:#FFF9F2;color:#000000;margin-bottom:0.3em;padding: 0 0.2em;border-radius: 0.1em">
Let July be July by Morgan Harper Nichols

Even here, you are growing.\
When August is approaching\
and you feel a little restless\
thinking about how\
this month might end\
and how\
this year might end\
and how you are supposed to\
start again,\
you are growing,\
you are growing,\
in grace\
courage\
strength.

And it is okay\
if it does not feel like it.\
It is okay if there are moments\
where you cannot see\
the way you have grown,\
because far beneath the surface\
the seeds have still been sown.\
The ground beneath your feet\
is still a bed for new beginnings.

So much is changing around you \
but you are changing, too.

You are so much more than the brokenness\
that you were certain would define you.

It has not been easy for you.\
You have worked so hard\
to be the positive one.\
You have given your best\
in areas of your life\
where the effort was not returned. \
And this has made it so hard\
for you to keep going,\
and there have been days\
where you were not sure\
if it was even possible.\
But after everything,\
here you are,\
just a little stronger,\
holding on a little longer,\
and you still found room for hope. 

So take heart\
breathe deep\
you are still becoming\
who you were meant to be.

Let July be July.\
Let August be August.\
And let yourself 

just be\
even in\
the uncertainty.\
You don’t have to fix\
everything.\
You don’t have solve\
everything.\
And you can still\
find peace\
and grow\
in the wild\
of changing things.</div>
</details> that was meaningful to her, and it was nice!</div>

<div style="background:#FFF9F2;color:#000000;margin-bottom:0.3em;padding: 0 0.2em;border-radius: 0.1em">
<strong>I saw a ghost in the living room</strong>

<em>From <a href="https://vndb.org/v19273">SeaBed</a>, paraphrased</em>

I saw a ghost in the living room.\
But as I was already used to the phenomenon, I did not let it interfere with the preparation of my evening meal.\
The ghost ate my fried egg and commended its taste.\
As I listened to her talk, my memories drifted back to the days we were still together.\
Back then, she asked me what was necessary for the two of us to be together.

Alone in that spacious living room, I attempted to figure out why it all ended this way. Why those days when nothing could stand in our way faded into the past.\
I no longer felt like I could do everything.\
The world had grown complicated. Even simple matters became difficult.\
The rules we had established in the past no longer seemed to apply, and the castle we built for ourselves crumbled to dust.\
"What is necessary for us to be together?" – the ghost asked me.\
We need a make a new place for ourselves.\
A place no one can destroy.\
Let's make it a place that no one else can reach.\
And so, I inconspicuously proceeded with the plan.\
In a place so deep below the surface that no one else can find it.</div>
</details>, but... Well, yeah; so as it turned out, she was actually encouraging me to share one of my own poems!</div>

Yeah! Me! In front of like; 10 people- in person; **reading something I'd written**! So I'm sitting there, dumbfounded and fumbling- oh, I don't end up reading any of my poetry to them, by the way... And I'm thinking to myself about a couple of key properties concerning the situation I'm in.

It's not like I'm not comfortable sharing my writing *in general*, right? I mean, I do heaps and heaps of technical writing on a regular basis, so that can't be it. Hell, even when it comes to publishing novice fanfiction when I was in high school or text-form roleplaying in public boards, both of which are *exceptionally* juvenile and humiliating... Sure, I had a little reservation. A *little.* But nothing like what I felt there that night with Mari and the crew.

And so, I had to give it some thought! I narrowed it down to two things that made it an impossible ask:
- I will not be laid bare for ridicule among a group of people I love. But more importantly:
- I will not take my work out of the context in which it was served for. Every poem of mine has a specific purpose. 

... If I ever share my poetry with her one day, Mari promised me she'd tell me the song she wrote before she made that painting.
