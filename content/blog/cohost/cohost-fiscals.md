I'm really glad about the latest good financial news!

<!--more-->

Weirdly, the published financials make me feel *less* anxious about the platform, because like... These are totally attainable, realistic goals. I've had accounts on other platforms that I knew vaguely probably weren't sustainable but kept hush hush about it, then... Boom. One day gone.

The hard-line stance on open-source is a bit odd to me. If the team needs to slim and start taking open-source contributors, I think it'd be just fine to use remaining revenue on content moderation, hosting, paying back loans, pull request reviews (yay), and regular development. Like, I know there's this big "ethics" sword of Damocles, but I don't really think accepting volunteer labor while you're getting paid is a sin or anything like that.

More importantly, I don't think it'd push anyone away from the site. Worst case, some jaded developers might just not contribute.

'Course, hopefully it won't come to that.
