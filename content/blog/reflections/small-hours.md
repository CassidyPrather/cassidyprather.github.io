Last night, due to a variety of circumstances, I had a bunch of Claude tokens on my subscription plan to burn. It was late and I was tired. Every moment I spent working on something would translate to a moment less of sleep. So, I decided to hop on the "let's see what the new model can do" bandwagon:

> Make a game (or game-ish) prototype you think I would appreciate. In addition to the guidance in the game-prototypes repo, I've included my website for context, where I've documented my taste extensively (though quite a lot of that is from when I was a naive, young kid!)
> 
> Deadline: Wed 5:00 AM (I bet you know why)

Fable 5.2 produced the game "Small Hours". It was not very good. In fact, dare I say, it was quite bad!
Well, actually, the puzzle at the end of the game was *okay*. It took me like 15 minutes to solve. It was a crazy difficulty spike, given the rest of the game was mind-numbingly easy! But I felt fulfilled at the end, so that's something.
Anyway, maybe this was a harsh test, but I still think there is a long road ahead before these things become competent at game development.

In addition to the gameplay itself being poor, the visuals and audio are not good either.

## Mitigations

I am extremely hesitant to write any solid list of mitigations, as I'm sure they'll be 100% obsolete by this time next year.
Still, for the sake of being thorough, a few things come to mind:

- Need to provide at least a list of style references. Pinterest may be dead but there are plenty of alternatives.
- Claude is addicted to synthetic noises and doesn't want to touch a sample library.
- Claude is terrible at extrapolating taste on its own (well as of today)
