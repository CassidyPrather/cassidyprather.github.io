<p><strong>Rating: 6.5/10</strong></p>
A cute solitaire game which is very pleasing in a tactile sense. The balance is more flavor than anything else, but that still has its own appeal to it. There is a lot of careful bookkeeping which needs to be done, and the "character sheets" can get pretty messy; it's also kind of easy to lose pieces. Still, this game holds a special place in my heart and childhood and will always be something I am happy to come back to.
</div>
