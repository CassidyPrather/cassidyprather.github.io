<p><strong>Rating: 6.5/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
A solid and entertaining dexterity game with a very loose ruleset. As long as you don't take it too seriously, the game will be fine. That being said, this sort of "casual spirit" is not an excuse for very lackluster design and callibration. It is especially notable that the asymmetry of the game, while fine at 2 players, deteriorates considerably at 3 players, and falls apart entirely at 4. 5 players is possible but would be inadvisable in my opinion.
</div>
