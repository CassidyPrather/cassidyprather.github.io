<p><strong>Rating: 5/10</strong></p>
A very over the top violent area control game which heavily relies on drafting into specific play styles. Although the combat is satisfying, the learning curve is extremely steep which makes the game somewhat inaccessible and lopsided for new players. The game often also feels like a very long, cumbersome process as the outcome of each individual actions tend to have marginal and arbitrary benefits which are difficult to assess with a grasp on the general flow of the game and hopeless without it.
