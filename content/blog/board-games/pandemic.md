<p><strong>Rating: 6.5/10</strong></p>
I'd probably enjoy this one a lot more if I wasn't afraid of germs. The game is very consistent in it's theme, which can be a good or a bad thing depending on your personal disposition. The game play is very simple and straightforward, and as such the game is brutally unforgiving. As in any good coop, players interact with each other a fair bit and try to exchange information and strategies in a fun, brainstorming environment.
