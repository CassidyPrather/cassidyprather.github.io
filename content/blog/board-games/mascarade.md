<p><strong>Rating: 7.8/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
Mascarade is built around a fairly simple concept, merging together social deduction and memory, but it's very satisfying to try to analyze the board state repeatedly. Player interaction . Mascarade suffers from very frequently placing players into positions where they have to "clean up" the player in the lead in order to prevent them from easily snowballing, something which would only vanish if all players had a very astute awareness of the board state at all times.
