<p><strong>Rating: 6/10</strong></p>
Dinosaur island involves very standard worker placement strategy with a sort of overbearing theme. There are numerous mechanics to keep track of, but doing so in of itself doesn't feel like an especially rewarding experience, and it makes the difficulty of keeping track of all of the other players somewhat intense. Still, it is pleasant to try to generate and follow through on strategies based on the rotating market.
