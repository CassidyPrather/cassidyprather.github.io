<p><strong>Rating: 5/10</strong> · Played 2 times</p>
Highly mechanical and aggressive, One Night Ultimate Werewolf incorporates a ton of different and disparate rules and player roles practically at random in an attempt to occasionally generate interesting interactions or combinations. While this might be okay for some groups, it is a very niche interest. At the very least, I am a big fan of the game's visuals, even though the theme is slightly annoying.
