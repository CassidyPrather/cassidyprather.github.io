<p><strong>Rating: 6.5/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
A cute, simple, and casual social deduction game which is extremely satisfying in the tactile sense. The mechanical construction of the "social deduction" elements are sort of weak and sporadic, but the very chaotic nature of the game makes it less noticeable. Thematically, Bang the Dice Game portrays a very uncomfortable, insensitive depiction of demographics which is typical of the "Wild West" genre.
