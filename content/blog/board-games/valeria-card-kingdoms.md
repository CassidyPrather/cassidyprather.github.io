<div class="review-text">
<p><strong>Rating: 5.5/10</strong></p>
Valeria is a decent dice-rolling game with a satisfying ramp, but ultimately feels rather bland and boring, especially on subsequent plays. Spending the games "red/attack" resource simply feels like a necessary chore, when I would rather just focus on the gold market. Still, Valeria is a good time killer.
