<p><strong>Rating: 2.6/10</strong></p>
Dull, uninteresting, and is based around an iterated introduction of mechanics; these are all death sentences for what is essential a party game. Mix that together with a clunky, slow turn-based team game where one person plays at a time and you have an absolutely miserable dexterity game experience.
