<p><strong>Rating: 1/10</strong></p>
Cards Against Humanity is an uncomfortable, miserable train-wreck of a party game. It relies heavily upon shock humor and saying or insinuating things which are wildly disconcerting or outright offensive. That being the "point" of the game does not make this remotely comfortable; it operates on an immature, dull fascination with the idea that "it's okay/funny because it's ironic." Avoid at all costs.
