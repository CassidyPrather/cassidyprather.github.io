<p><strong>Rating: 5.8/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
A pretty straightforward worker placement game that hinges on very high aggression and tight racing to fulfill scoring brackets. The game's complexity seems to be just an excuse for it's frustrating scoring system, but it's still fun to play nevertheless. From an aesthetic point of view, the game is extremely ugly to the point of me being considerably less prone to play it.
