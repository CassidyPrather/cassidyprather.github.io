<div class="review-text">
<p><strong>Rating: 3/10</strong></p>
A boring game which tries to tell the players to have fun, rather than presenting interesting mechanics. The art style is atrocious; everything about the game tries to be funny and just isn't. At all. The gendered humor is also uncomfortable. Munchkin operates on selling as many copies and expansions as possible, avoid it if you can.
</div>
