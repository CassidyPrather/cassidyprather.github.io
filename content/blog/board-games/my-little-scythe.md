<div class="review-text">
<p><strong>Rating: 7/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
A super sugary, pastel, and adorable area control game. The mechanics are very simple and accessible, although they feel arbitrary at times. The game permeates a very warm and friendly atmosphere between players and doesn't command a terrible amount of attention to enjoy fully. The figures are especially pretty, and the main draw is the nice child-friendly setting.
