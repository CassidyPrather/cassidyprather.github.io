<p><strong>Rating: 6.2/10</strong> · Played 2 times</p>
Mystic Veil is extremely interesting and satisfying to execute, but suffers from a nearly total absence of any player interaction whatsoever. It baffles me how such a massive oversight could plague a game produced at such an outstanding quality, and it frustrates me to no end because I really want to like this. As it stands, while I would probably be willing to play this pretty often, I just would rather do so many other things with my time.<br>
It's pretty at least, and feels extremely rewarding. I can't deny that it's very straightforward though.
