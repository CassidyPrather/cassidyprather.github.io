<div class="review-text">
<p><strong>Rating: 3.7/10</strong></p>
Dull and frustrating deck builder. While the idea of interconnecting board and market mechanics sound interesting, the physical racing of the game feels sort of mechanical and bland. The game plays too slowly, especially since Clank is pretty prone to dud hands, which isn't normally too much of a problem in deck-builders. Even the draw bag is physically uncomfortable, ruining what otherwise might have been a fun physical drawing process, and the pieces are far too easy to lose track of and knock all over the place. Overall, just boring.
</div>
