<div class="review-text">
<p><strong>Rating: 5/10</strong></p>
Rather than being tactility satisfying, building the castle from the market felt sort of mechanical and boring. Player interaction was extremely limited, and it's rare for anybody to actually interact through the market. It's a whole bunch of procedural analytical games of solitaire all being played at once. There are cute parts about the aesthetic but the mediocre art quality and sort of bland sense of humor made it pretty unmemorable.
