<p><strong>Rating: 8.6/10</strong> · Played 6 times</p>
An absolutely beautifully executed strategy game which is extremely well paced and rewards a wide variety of strategies. Despite having a steep learning curve, the game is fun to pick up and play for the first time, since experimenting with tactics is so much fun. The game is also aesthetically beautiful and extremely satisfying from a tactile perspective.
