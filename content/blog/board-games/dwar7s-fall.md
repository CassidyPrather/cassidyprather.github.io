<p><strong>Rating: 7/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
A very high interaction game with a very steep learning curve and moderate skill cap. There's always a lot to think about and a wide variety of options, and the player interaction is very fluid and dynamic. The aesthetic of the game is mediocre, it tries to pull off a very whimsical silly setting but ended up looking pretty bloated.
