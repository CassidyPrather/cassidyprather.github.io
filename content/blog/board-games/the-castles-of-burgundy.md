<p><strong>Rating: 6/10</strong></p>
A fairly simple base construction game involving a lot of keeping track of dice and separate resource pools. Feels a bit too much like several concurrent games of solitaire for comfort, especially since the biggest extent to which player interaction is relevant is in other players acting as timers of various speeds in various mechanical senses.
