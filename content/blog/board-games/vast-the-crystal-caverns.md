<p><strong>Rating: 7.3/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
Vast is extremely asymmetrical and balanced around keeping a close eye on each of your opponents individually, encouraging deep and meaningful player interactions between everyone at the table. As a consequence of this, Vast can get pretty aggressive as well. Each play-style is extremely distinct, allowing for a lot of intersecting player values to come together in the same game for a rewarding experience. Aesthetically, the game is extremely pretty and cute, leaving me with a fuzzy warm feeling after playing.
