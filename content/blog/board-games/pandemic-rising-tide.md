<p><strong>Rating: 6.5/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
A super fun theme of pandemic with a lot of interesting decision making opportunities. Pandemic Rising Tide requires a lot of comfort with some very high focus on the player-drawn cards within the game, but there are usually plenty of interesting options for turn nevertheless. I especially like the sort of area control / compartmentalization type of strategy, and although I'm sure it would get bothersome eventually I have been very entertained by the novelty of it.
