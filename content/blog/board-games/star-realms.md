<p><strong>Rating: 7/10</strong> · Played 3 times</p>
Star Realms is a pretty, straightforward deck builder most notable for its portability. individual expansion packs can tweak the game slightly in interesting ways, but putting them all together sacrifices the low weight and size which is the core appeal of the game over others.<br>
For me personally, Star Realms is an excellent choice to carry around in my bag with me everywhere I go, as depending on the person I am with it can be a very pleasant and satisfying experience to play the game. I would not make any efforts to plan to play Star Realms more than a few hours out, though.
