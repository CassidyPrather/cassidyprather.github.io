<p><strong>Rating: 8/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
While this game is very complicated and is centered around a very rigid numerical scoring system, it leads to some fantastic player interaction and luck pushing. It is one of the most detailed competitive worker placement games I have ever played, especially considering that player hostility takes a very mechanically engaging form. The main issue with the game is a frustrating and boring scoring system; there is no engaging sort of win condition.
