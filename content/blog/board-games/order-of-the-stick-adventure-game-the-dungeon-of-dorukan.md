<p><strong>Rating: 6/10</strong></p>
A love letter to the Order of the Stick webcomic. The play is surprisingly slick for what is effectively the board game equivalent of shovelware, but the proper place for aggression is unclear leading to a sort of clunky overall experience. The humor dies past the first play through, but the mechanics are solid enough to warrant coming back nevertheless.
