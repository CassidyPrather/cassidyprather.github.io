<p><strong>Rating: 3/10</strong></p>
Although Zombies has some good ideas, it falls flat and the strategically valuable choices in the game involve entirely circumventing its core mechanics. There is no intuitive sense or interaction between different mechanics and trends. Also involves repeatedly running into the same boring exploration task over and over again. Cards also don't provide much strategic value.
