<p><strong>Rating: 5.3/10</strong> · Played 4 times</p>
Gloomhaven is a very slow and poorly balanced experience. The intense RPG mechanics turn what might otherwise have been a very interesting tactical fighter into a scripted slog. Difficulty is managed in a very boring way, mainly consisting of the inflation of numbers and dropping of meaningless, frustrating, and repetitive minis all over the board. Gloomhaven has yet to present me with a uniquely interesting challenge or turn where my decisions didn't feel vague, nebulous, or pointless.<br>
I am personally very dissapointed in the inter-player communication rules; while I am a fan of games which restrict peoples' way to communicate in creative and interesting ways, Gloomhaven's system is just annoying and vague.<br>
I also don't feel particularly motivated to engage with the game's long term progression system, since it doesn't seem like it will push the game in any fun or creative ways.<br>
Overall, I like the idea of gloomhaven a ton and I want to keep looking for things about it I will enjoy, but I can't get behind the execution.
