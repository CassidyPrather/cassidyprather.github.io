<p><strong>Rating: 6/10</strong></p>
A lot of the mechanics of Mysterium are counterintuitive, which is frustrating for a game with such a simple picture-association premise. Nevertheless, it really stretches the imagination in fun and creative ways and can be a good way to experiment with unconventional methods of communication.
