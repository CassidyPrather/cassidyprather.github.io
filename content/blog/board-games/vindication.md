<p><strong>Rating: 7.5/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
A fun and beautiful game with a lot of nods to traditional design principles with modern tuning sense. The game definitely suffers quite a bit from its boring victory conditions and very high amount of per-player downtime, but it makes up for it with a very satisfying core game-play loop and being very strategically accessible.
