<div class="review-text">
<p><strong>Rating: 7/10</strong></p>
A very lighthearted hex-grid based fighter game. GKR doesn't borrow many strategic elements from other games, making learning it a unique experience. High player counts can lead to an increased level of table aggression. While the game is not inaccessible, it is a considerable investment to play for the first time, and the skill ceiling is low.
</div>
