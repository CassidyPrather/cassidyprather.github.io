<p><strong>Rating: 6/10</strong></p>
Machi Koro is a really cute dice rolling game with an intensely satisfying player progression. There is hardly anything at all to the strategy though; easy learning curve and low skill cap. Machi Koro has more player interaction than I would have expected from a "everybody's building their own thing from a market' game, but it still isn't much.
