<p><strong>Rating: 7/10</strong> · Played 6 times</p>
A solid, fast paced, entry level social deduction game which is easy to deploy in a very wide variety of group dynamics. While the rotating card market provides variety, if the game is not played very often it might as well be substituted for the original "Coup" game which gets more interesting mechanics for having a fixed deck. Coup can also become very aggressive, as although there are no teams there are often times a series of choices where multiple harmful actions to other players produce the same benefit.
