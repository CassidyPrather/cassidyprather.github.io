<p><strong>Rating: 8.5/10</strong></p>
A fun combination of social deduction and word association. This game challenges players to think extremely critically about communication and patterns in an upbeat, fast paced context. By adding elements of deception to social deduction, Murder in Hong explores what happens when people try to deliberately find patterns which do not exist, which is a fun and exciting twist.
