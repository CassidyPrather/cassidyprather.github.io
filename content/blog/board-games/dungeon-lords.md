<p><strong>Rating: 9/10</strong> · Played 1 time</p>
Dungeon Lords turns what would otherwise be several fun concurrent games of solitaire into a brilliant, social interaction with an excellent application of worker placement mechanics. The core mechanic of the game revolves around players reducing the difficulty of the "puzzle" that they have to solve (which occurs twice during the game).
