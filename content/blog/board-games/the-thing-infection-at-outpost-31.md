<p><strong>Rating: 3/10</strong> · Played 2 times</p>
While there is a certain sort of appeal to the IDEA of this game, the execution is extremely flawed, both in terms of mechanics and the ergonomics of the game. The learning curve is very steep and then plateues a little bit too far in to be comfortable for a social deduction game. It is also very boring, with single games going on for ages despite the fact that decisive advantages can be claimed early on. The balance of the game is rather arbitrary and burdened by needlessly complex mechanics. Lastly, from an aesthetic point of view, the game is just really dull and frustrating, even considering the good quality of the minis.
