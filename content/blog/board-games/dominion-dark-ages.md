<div class="review-text">
<p><strong>Rating: 8/10</strong></p>
Dark Ages adds a ton of extremely interesting cards to Dominion, some of which are just plain hilarious (I'm looking at you, rats). The shelters dramatically increase the complexity of the game in its early phases, which is a breath of fresh air after many games with the default starter decks. Ruins are also a far more interesting "junk" card than curses are.
