<p><strong>Rating: 7/10</strong> · Played 4 times</p>
A decent and cute time killer which is easy to learn and allows for a bit of creativity. Unfortunately, the skill cap is very high and small disparities in skill can result in massive win prospects, but since the game is very satisfying from a tactile perspective and the majority of the game consists of interesting puzzles, it remains enjoyable nevertheless.
